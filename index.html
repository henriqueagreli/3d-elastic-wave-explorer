Flexural pulse dispersion

For a thin plate, bending moment depends on curvature. Curvature contributes two spatial derivatives and force balance contributes two more, giving a fourth-order term: D∇⁴w. Therefore ω²=(D/ρh)k⁴, so ω=√(D/ρh)k². Higher-k components have larger phase and group velocities, causing a pulse to spread.
